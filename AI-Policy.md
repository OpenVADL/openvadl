# OpenVADL AI Policy

- **Communication is no place for AI.** Emails, Matrix, Issues and Discussions are meant for humans to interact with humans. Don't be a [meat-proxy](https://meatproxy.me/). Everyone has access to generative AI, there is never a need for _"Codex/Claude/Gemini says [...]"_. You may have gotten an idea from an LLM, but it is only a meaningful contribution if you vetted it and are of the same opinion.

- **The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code.** Contributors are fully responsible for the code they submit. If someone asks why it was done that way, you have to be able to justify the chosen approach. Hold any code you submit to the same standard as your own, hand-written work. The person reviewing your contribution is taking time out of their schedule for you, please value it.

- **Documentation is for Humans.** It is also hard to write and often an afterthought. Unvetted generated docs only add fluff, no value; The reader has access to an LLM as well. Generated docs that are vetted by the author *can* be better than none. Hand written docs, possibly closing with a pun or meme, are however preferred. Here be dragons and increment that hours spent counter please! In particular, AI can be quite verbose to the point of being confusing, so try to avoid that.

- **We trust each other.** These rules are not intended to be policed. We trust that everyone will recognize them as a basis for respectful community interactions and thus sufficiently adhere to them.

- **Do what you want outside the official `openvadl` repo.** Vibe coded tooling can be quite helpful, whether it is for meeting recording, translation, benchmark suite or any other visualization.



This policy was written and is maintained by humans.
